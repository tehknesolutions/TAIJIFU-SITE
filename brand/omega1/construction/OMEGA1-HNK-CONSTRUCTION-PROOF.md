## Three-gesture synthesis

### AXIS
Carries the martial vertical shared explicitly by G22, G01, G03 and G36. It is the dominant orientation and prevents Ω1 from becoming a generic circular martial-arts symbol.

Abstracts the diamond/containment ancestry of G22 and G25 into an open adaptive field rather than reproducing a literal diamond. The opening is intentional: adaptation is not represented as a sealed system.

### FLOW
Abstracts the strongest continuous curve family from G25/G05, informed by the alternating curves of G01/G03. It crosses and returns around the AXIS without mirror symmetry.
