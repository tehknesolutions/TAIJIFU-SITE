# TAIJIFU Official Brand System — Design Specification

**Status:** APPROVED DESIGN / IMPLEMENTATION SPEC
**Date:** 2026-09-23
**Parent identity:** TAIJIFU Ω1 — ORIGIN × NEXUS × FLOW

## 1. Intent

Build a memorable, production-grade TAIJIFU identity system that can govern the website, app, uniforms, documents, physical marks and ceremonial/editorial material. The experience must feel like entering a serious contemporary dojo: strength, intelligence and adaptation, with HNK embedded structurally rather than used as decoration.

## 2. Canonical core

The official emblem is Ω1. Its production geometry is already frozen as:

- `brand/omega1/master/omega1-master.svg` for >= 32 px;
- `brand/omega1/master/omega1-micro-master.svg` for < 32 px.

Ω1 genealogy remains:

`G22 · G01 · G03 · G36 · G03 · G25 · G05`

The construction principle remains:

`ORIGIN × NEXUS × FLOW → Ω1`

## 3. Brand character

The system combines:

- martial seriousness;
- disciplined geometry;
- controlled human gesture;
- intelligence and clarity;
- adaptive movement;
- contemporary precision;
- Japanese-influenced restraint through proportion, void, rhythm and materiality rather than clichés.

Explicitly avoid generic ninja/samurai typography, fake kanji, decorative katakana, permanent neon, gamer glow, rainbow treatment and gradients as identity foundations.

## 4. Proprietary wordmark

The verbal mark is always **TAIJIFU** in uppercase.

It is a proprietary lettering system, not a commercial font used as the primary identity.

Personality ratio:

- 50% geometric precision;
- 50% controlled martial gesture.

Internal rhythm is perceived as:

`TAI | JI | FU`

without literal spaces in the everyday wordmark.

### Letter anatomy

- **T — Decision / Axis:** dominant vertical and firm upper opening.
- **A — Origin / Ascent:** cut apex and distinctive internal negative space.
- **I — Clarity:** minimal vertical pause.
- **J — Adaptation:** begins structurally and resolves into a controlled curve/hook.
- **I — Equilibrium:** restores vertical balance after J.
- **F — Strength / Filter:** rigid structure with an asymmetric martial cut.
- **U — Flow / Truth:** most organic closing curve in the sequence.

### Shared genetic features

Every letter must use the same family of:

1. structural axes;
2. martial diagonal cuts;
3. controlled gestural terminals.

The HNK relationship is discoverable in construction details but must never reduce immediate Latin readability.

## 5. Proportion system

Wordmark construction uses a normalized 1000-unit cap-height design field.

In the primary horizontal lockup, Ω1 has approximately **1.35× the optical height of the wordmark**.

Spacing is optical rather than mechanically uniform. TAI, JI and FU have distinguishable rhythms while remaining one continuous word.

## 6. Lockup architecture

### Primary horizontal

`Ω1  TAIJIFU`

Primary use: website header, uniforms, documents, product UI and general brand communication.

### Ceremonial vertical

Ω1 above TAIJIFU.

Primary use: covers, dojo entrance, seals, posters and ceremonial applications.

### Expanded origin signature

Ω1 + TAIJIFU + HNK source sequence/signature.

Reserved for editorial, educational, construction-proof and ceremonial contexts. It is not the default website header.

### Symbol-only

Ω1 may stand alone after sufficient brand context or where space requires it.

## 7. Color architecture

The identity must remain complete in one color. Black/white is the authority test.

Semantic accent roles:

- **TAI:** red;
- **JI:** blue;
- **FU:** gold/yellow;
- **Integration / Survival:** green.

The everyday wordmark must not become a mandatory four-color/rainbow object. Semantic color is an optional communication layer.

Exact HEX/RGB/CMYK production values are calibrated during implementation and documented as tokens; they must preserve contrast and material reproduction.

## 8. Material language

Preferred material references:

- ink;
- paper/fiber;
- dark wood;
- steel;
- uniform textile;
- stone;
- controlled natural light.

Technology is communicated through precision, responsive behavior, interaction and motion — not through neon decoration.

## 9. Responsive identity

- >= 32 px: Ω1 standard master;
- < 32 px: Ω1 micro master;
- wide contexts: horizontal lockup;
- narrow/ceremonial contexts: vertical lockup;
- tiny UI contexts: micro symbol only.

No implementation may simply shrink a ceremonial lockup until text/details become illegible.

## 10. Motion identity

The official reveal may use:

`G22 → G01 → G03 → G36 → G03 → G25 → G05 → convergence → Ω1`

Motion is short, disciplined and intentional. Avoid particle spectacle, magical explosions and decorative effects that obscure construction.

## 11. Required production assets

The implementation phase produces:

- proprietary TAIJIFU wordmark master;
- horizontal master lockup;
- vertical master lockup;
- HNK expanded signature;
- monochrome and reverse variants;
- semantic-accent variant;
- favicon set;
- app-icon set;
- seal/embroidery-safe variant;
- identity design tokens;
- construction/proportion documentation;
- application previews for web header, mobile/app, uniform and dojo/ceremonial surface.

## 12. Validation

No asset is promoted to master without:

- immediate TAIJIFU readability;
- Ω1 geometry unchanged from canonical master;
- mono and reverse verification;
- responsive-size verification;
- clear-space verification;
- lockup proportion verification;
- no accidental clipping/self-intersection;
- semantic color contrast check;
- favicon/app-icon check;
- embroidery/single-color viability;
- HNK genealogy retained without invented glyphs.

## 13. Site identity consequence

The future website UI is designed from this brand system rather than forcing the brand into an existing page template. The target experience is **entering the TAIJIFU dojo**, not viewing a slide-site about martial arts.

The site should derive spacing, materials, typography hierarchy, motion restraint and semantic accents from this system. It must avoid the previously rejected neon/gradient/Wix-like/PowerPoint-like presentation.

## 14. Design lock

Approved decisions:

- Ω1 is the official emblem;
- proprietary wordmark direction D;
- uppercase `TAIJIFU`;
- 50/50 geometry and martial gesture;
- internal rhythm `TAI | JI | FU`;
- Ω1 horizontal dominance ratio ≈ 1.35× wordmark optical height;
- monochrome-first identity;
- red/blue/gold/green semantic system;
- HNK as structural/discoverable genealogy;
- contemporary serious dojo material language.

Changes to these locks require a new explicit design decision.
