# TAIJIFU — Canon Authority

Status: ACTIVE
Effective: 2026-09-29

## Authority

`tehknesolutions/TAIJIFU-SITE` is the official and sole TAIJIFU repository.

This repository is the authoritative home for:

- TAIJIFU Canon;
- official doctrine and curriculum;
- official public content;
- information architecture;
- brand identity and visual rules;
- official assets;
- product specifications;
- web and interactive implementations;
- tests and executable projections;
- recovered legacy material and its provenance;
- project archaeology and reconciliation records.

Historical references that identify another TAIJIFU repository as Source of Truth are legacy architecture and MUST NOT be interpreted as current authority.

## Consolidation rule

All recoverable TAIJIFU material that existed before the current repository is to be consolidated here with provenance preserved.

Migration does not silently promote every historical statement to current Canon. Recovered material is classified before incorporation as one of:

- `CANON` — current official TAIJIFU truth;
- `OFFICIAL_PRODUCT` — official product/design/runtime rule that does not redefine Canon;
- `SOURCE` — primary source retained for reconciliation;
- `EVIDENCE` — material supporting a historical or product claim;
- `LEGACY` — superseded implementation or historical state;
- `EXPERIMENTAL` — exploration not promoted to Canon;
- `UNRESOLVED` — conflict or gap requiring explicit reconciliation.
