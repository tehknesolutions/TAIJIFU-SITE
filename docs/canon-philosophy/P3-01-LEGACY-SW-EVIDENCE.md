## PHIL-SRC-0002 — M001 Manifesto SimpleWay Taijifu

Locator: `tehknesolutions/SW-TAIJIFU:docs/M001-manifesto.md@d4a8bf09ad05e7b9ef5789f008d4a7ab2e39bde0`
Class: `LEGACY_PRODUCT`
Scope: SimpleWay Taijifu teaching/product layer.

Bounded evidence supported by the source:

- SimpleWay Taijifu is described as the teaching, practice, and evolution layer of the Taijifu system.
- Taijifu is described there as the physical and martial system.
- SimpleWay Taijifu turns that system into a teachable, measurable, progressive journey.
- The source names a central principle: learn fundamentals, practice, measure, correct, and advance.
- The curriculum is described as arising from real practice and progressive validation rather than remaining only theoretical.

Governance note: this is a legacy product manifesto. Its statements are candidates for reconciliation, not automatic statements of the current Taijifu philosophical Canon.

## PHIL-SRC-0003 — M004 Fundamental Principles

Governance note: these principles are valid evidence of the legacy SimpleWay Taijifu product/training model. P3-01 does not yet establish that all ten are philosophical Canon of Taijifu itself.
