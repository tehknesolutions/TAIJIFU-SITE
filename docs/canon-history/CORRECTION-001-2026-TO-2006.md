# CORRECTION-001 — `Desde 2026` → `Desde 2006`

## Conflict

Canon Visual V1 / Issue #7 contained the signature:

`Criado por Miguel Da Vinci e Thales Walisson — Desde 2026`

During EPIC-002, the creator clarified that an earlier official record — Thales Walisson born in 1992 and the origin at age 14 — was specifically supplied to define **Desde**.

## Resolution

The historical origin marker is corrected to:

`TAIJIFU — Desde 2006`

The calculation is year-level only: `1992 + 14 = 2006`.

## Preservation

The old 2026 wording is not erased from provenance. It remains historical evidence of the modern repository/Canon phase and is marked superseded **only as an origin marker**.

## Propagation debt

Any official UI, documentation, metadata, theme, plugin, Academy, Masters or downstream product that displays `Desde 2026` as TAIJIFU origin must eventually consume the corrected Canon value `Desde 2006`.
