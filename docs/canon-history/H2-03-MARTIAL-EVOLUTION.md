# H2-03 — Martial Evolution

Status: IN_PROGRESS
Epic: EPIC-002 — Canon Histórico
Authority: `tehknesolutions/TAIJIFU-SITE`

## Objective

Document the historical martial evolution of TAIJIFU without reconstructing lineage from resemblance, gameplay, terminology or general martial-arts knowledge.

## Current evidence state

The project history contains the creator-level assertion that TAIJIFU evolved through multiple martial arts, but the currently promoted repository evidence does not yet enumerate a source-backed list, chronology, teachers, schools or periods for those influences.

Therefore the broad assertion is tracked as a **candidate/unresolved historical claim**, not yet as a detailed canonical lineage.

## Required record shape

Each promoted influence should carry, where supported:

- martial art / system name;
- relationship type (`influence`, `study`, `practice`, `lineage`, or another explicitly ruled term);
- person/entity involved;
- date or period precision, when known;
- source locator or explicit creator ruling;
- conflict state;
- claim IDs used by the historical timeline.

Unknown fields remain unknown. They are not reconstructed.

## Separation rule

The following are not historical evidence by themselves:

- visual or mechanical similarity;
- current TAIJIFU curriculum structure;
- TAIJIFU Masters gameplay data;
- external descriptions of martial arts;
- assistant inference.

## Gate

`H2-03=PASS` requires at least one explicitly sourced martial-evolution record and an explicit treatment of the broader multi-art evolution claim.

Until then:

`H2-03=IN_PROGRESS`
