## Supported relationship

The current Canon verifies a semantic/genealogical relationship between HNK and TAIJIFU through `HIST-CLAIM-003` and its repository source.

That relationship is evidence of meaning and connection. It is **not**, by itself, evidence of a date, ordering or historical period.

## Chronology boundary

No chronological event is promoted in this increment.

The following remain unresolved:

- when HNK enters the TAIJIFU historical line;
- whether a particular HNK phase precedes or follows a particular TAIJIFU phase;
- which HNK events, if any, belong in the official TAIJIFU timeline;
- the dated historical meaning of the already verified semantic genealogy.

## Evidence rule

Repository creation dates, commit dates, product releases and implementation chronology may be useful provenance, but they do not automatically become dates for the underlying historical/philosophical relationship.

A chronological HNK claim requires either:

1. a stable source locator explicitly stating the historical date/period; or
2. an explicit creator ruling.
