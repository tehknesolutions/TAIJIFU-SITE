## Source basis

This classification is based on the project-provided legacy materials `Análise Taijifu Masters.txt` and `Relatório Taijifu Masters.txt`.

These materials are evidence of a concrete **TAIJIFU Masters game-production workflow**, but they do not identify a current canonical GitHub repository or prove a deployed Masters product location.

The materials explicitly describe:

- a Godot-based runtime validation workflow;
- `FighterController` integration;
- a character named **Lian Wu**;
- a **Training Rival**;
- a `VM01-A1 — Lian Wu Character Lock` production gate;
- transparent individual PNG character assets;
- bottom-center pivot, facing/flip, hitbox scale and contact-shadow validation;
- a required 1920×1080 in-engine screenshot;
- a procedural fallback replacement gate;
- a `PACK 04 — Reaction Frames` asset-production plan;
- 17 reaction frames for Lian Wu and 17 for Training Rival (34 total);
- reaction states `block_recoil`, `parry`, `posture_break`, `knockback`, and `neutral_recovery`.

## Canon boundary

The items above establish **product/gameplay/asset-pipeline evidence**. They do not, by themselves, establish historical Canon.

Specifically, the legacy Masters materials MUST NOT be used alone to infer:

- TAIJIFU historical origin;
- creator biography;
- martial lineage or historical martial influences;
- HNK chronology;
- Academy curriculum authority;
- historical dates for TAIJIFU.
